# Divergence and Curl

We just learned about the **Jacobian Matrix**, which contains all the partial derivatives of a vector field.
$$
J_{\mathbf{F}} =
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial y} \\
\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial y}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

While the Jacobian gives us the *full* picture of local change, it is often too much information. We want to summarize it into simpler concepts.
We can break the Jacobian down into two main "stories": **Divergence** and **Curl**.

## 1. Divergence (The Trace)

Divergence measures how much the vector field is **expanding** or **compressing** at a point.
Mathematically, it is the **trace** of the Jacobian (the sum of the diagonal elements).

$$
\text{div}(\mathbf{F}) = \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial y}
$$

*   **Positive Divergence:** The field is acting like a **Source** (stuff is being created or spreading out).
*   **Negative Divergence:** The field is acting like a **Sink** (stuff is being destroyed or compressing).
*   **Zero Divergence:** The field is incompressible (like water flowing in a pipe).

## 2. Curl (The Off-Diagonals)

Curl measures how much the vector field is **rotating** or **twisting** at a point.
In 2D, it is related to the difference of the off-diagonal elements.

$$
\text{curl}(\mathbf{F}) = \nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial y}
$$

*   **Positive Curl:** Counter-clockwise rotation.
*   **Negative Curl:** Clockwise rotation.
*   **Zero Curl:** Irrotational field (no local spinning).

> [!NOTE]
> **Is Jacobian different from Divergence?**
> Yes! The Jacobian is the **parent container**.
> *   The Jacobian is a **Matrix** (4 numbers in 2D).
> *   Divergence is a **Scalar** (1 number) derived from the Jacobian.
> *   Curl is a **Vector** (or scalar in 2D) derived from the Jacobian.
>
> You can think of Divergence and Curl as "summary statistics" of the Jacobian.

### Key Takeaways
1.  **Source:** $J = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$. Diagonals are positive $\to$ High Divergence.
2.  **Vortex:** $J = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$. Diagonals are zero $\to$ Zero Divergence. Off-diagonals are opposite $\to$ High Curl.
